Carrot

Carrot is an assistant that runs on your computer. Your chats, notes and files stay here unless you choose otherwise.

There are two ways to give it a brain. You can run a model on this machine, or connect to one over the internet. You can change this later, and you can use both.

How would you like to run Carrot?

Use the subscription you already pay for

If you have Claude Pro or Max, or ChatGPT Plus or Pro, you can sign in with that account rather than creating a second, separately-billed developer one.

How this works, precisely:

  • Carrot uses the provider's own sign-in page, opened in your real browser. It never sees your password.
  • It does not read your browser's cookies or drive the web app behind your back. Those methods break constantly and violate the providers' terms.
  • Sign-in needs this installation's OAuth client details. If your copy of Carrot does not have them yet, it will say so plainly instead of half-working — an API key is the reliable path until then.

What an API key is

An API key is a long password from a company that runs large AI models — Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and others. You paste it into Carrot once, and Carrot can then send your questions to their model and get answers back.

What it means in practice:

  • You pay for what you use, usually a fraction of a cent per question. Most providers ask for a card and let you set a spending cap. A heavy day of use is typically a few dollars, not hundreds.
  • Your questions leave this computer. They go to that company's servers to be answered. That is the trade for a much stronger model.
  • It is not the same as a chat subscription. Paying for Claude.ai or ChatGPT Plus does not give you an API key — they are separate products on separate billing. Carrot can use a subscription directly instead; that is the third option on the previous screen.
  • Treat it like a password. Anyone who has it can spend your money. Carrot stores it on this computer only.

You do not need one. Running locally is free and private, and you can add a key later from Settings whenever you want.

Pick who you have an account with. The link opens their key page; Carrot checks the key before saving it.

          Anthropic and OpenAI can be reached two ways. A developer API key is

          plan you may already pay for — Claude Pro/Max, ChatGPT Plus/Pro — through their own

          provider offers no sign-in flow, it says so rather than working around it.

Consensus & debate

        

        

          For questions where one confident paragraph is not enough. Two or more models
          answer independently, then critique each other's answers anonymously, then one
          writes the final answer — stating where the panel disagreed rather than
          averaging it away. Each model searches for its own evidence, so two of them
          finding different sources is a real disagreement rather than a difference in
          what they happened to memorise. Pick models that fail differently: a coding model
          and a reasoning model beat two of the same family. It costs roughly as many
          tokens and as much time as models on the panel, so it is per-question rather
          than a mode you leave on.
        

        

        

        

          
          
        

        

          
          
        

      


      
      

        

          
          
What Carrot is allowed to see

        

        

          Ambient capture — reading your screen so you can ask "what was that thing I
          had open on Tuesday" — is off, and every one of these rules is on before you
          arrive. You should never have to think to exclude a private browsing window:
          opening one is you saying, in the only words the browser gave you, that this
          is not to be kept. Nothing here is capture. This is the gate capture has to
          get through, and you can try it below on a window that does not exist.

Local webhooks

        

        

          Let Home Assistant, a Stream Deck macro, a Shortcut or a shell script on your
          own network talk to Carrot. This is the only way into the app that does not
